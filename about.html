This paragraph is yours: where you’re from, what brought you to making things on the web, and what keeps you curious. Write it the way you’d tell a friend over coffee — three or four sentences is plenty.

Another paragraph for the middle of the story: what you work on now, the kind of problems you enjoy, and the odd non-obvious skill you picked up somewhere unexpected.

Close with where you’d like this to go. A sentence about direction reads better than a list of ambitions.
